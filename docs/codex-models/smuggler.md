# The Smuggler Sai (`smuggler.glb`)

Role: witness quest (boars). Age 30, 1.76 m, wiry, slouched, sly grin, messy hair.

Outfit: layered jacket with many pockets and straps, scarf, fingerless gloves, eye
patch on the right eye (he lifts it when lying — separate mesh `Prop_EyePatch` on the
head so it can be animated), soft boots, small satchel. Palette: brown, sand, dirty teal.

Prop: coin (`Prop_Coin`, right hand slot).

Idle_Character: flips the coin and catches it, glances around.
Interact: leans in and lifts the eye patch. Budget 8 000 triangles.
