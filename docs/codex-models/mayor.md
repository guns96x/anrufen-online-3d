# Mayor Varen (`mayor.glb`)

Role: quest giver for the opening (town hall square). Age 60, 1.78 m, heavy-set, barrel
chest, slightly stooped. Grey beard braided into two plaits, receding hair tied back,
round spectacles on a chain.

Outfit: long olive waistcoat with the town crest (stylised oak leaf over a gate)
embroidered on the chest, linen shirt with rolled sleeves, wide belt with keys, dark
trousers, worn leather shoes. Palette: chestnut, olive, copper.

Prop: thick ledger book carried under the left arm (separate mesh `Prop_Ledger`
parented to the left hand slot).

Idle_Character: adjusts the spectacles with a finger, opens the ledger, sighs.
Interact gesture: points at the ledger page. Budget 8 000 triangles.
