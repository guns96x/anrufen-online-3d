# The Gourmand Bruno (`gourmand.glb`)

Role: witness quest (bandits) and the cooking chain. Age 45, 1.75 m, round and massive,
rosy cheeks, curly hair under a small cook's cap, big moustache.

Outfit: stained apron over a striped shirt, rolled sleeves, broad sash, short trousers,
clogs. Huge wooden ladle tucked in the sash (`Prop_Ladle`, parented to Hips), a
string of sausages over one shoulder. Palette: ochre, white, tomato red.

Idle_Character: tastes from the ladle, chews, pats belly, nods happily.
Interact: waves the ladle while talking. Budget 8 000 triangles.
