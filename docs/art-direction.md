# Guanlin presentation, version 0.2

The first location uses warm plaster, oak frames, teal slate and a green forest.
Gameplay remains a third-person, landscape, touch-first fantasy RPG.

## Production

`tools/build_village_v2.py` creates the actual GLB assets under `assets/models/v2`.
Run with Blender 5.0.1, `--background --python tools/build_village_v2.py`.
Original models are retained. One unit is one metre. Models have ground pivots.
Buildings use UV mapped materials. Character limb pivots drive locomotion and
weapon swings in `character_motion.gd`. Character and tree colours are stored
in vertex palettes to reduce material surfaces. Tree borders use MultiMesh.
Asset geometry counts are recorded in `assets/models/v2/budgets.json`.

The material atlas was generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. Its saved
project path is `assets/textures/village_atlas.png`; four material crops are
`cobble.png`, `plaster.png`, `wood.png`, `slate.png` in the same directory.
The crops are 1024 pixels square. The original generated atlas is also retained.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual game material texture atlas for
a stylized 3D fantasy village, NOT a screenshot or concept scene. Generate ONE
square 2048x2048 image divided into EXACTLY four equal square quadrants, no
gutters, labels, borders, text or perspective. Each quadrant is a flat seamless
material viewed straight-on with diffuse neutral illumination and no cast
shadows. Top left: weathered warm grey medieval cobblestone paving, irregular
fitted small granite stones, subtle moss in narrow joints, broad hand-painted
shapes. Top right: pale warm ivory lime plaster, subtle trowel strokes and age
speckles, no objects. Bottom left: dark honey-brown oak wood planks, vertical
grain, subtle knots, restrained contrast, no nails. Bottom right: overlapping
desaturated deep teal blue slate roof tiles, neat staggered horizontal rows,
weathered edges. Consistent premium hand-painted fantasy RPG material art,
tactile, readable at mobile scale. Fill each quadrant edge to edge. All details
must be intrinsic surface color, not directional lighting.

## Runtime presentation

Ground mixes grass, cobbles and the forest trail in world space. Grass has a
vertex wind shader; fountain water has moving ripples. Mountain silhouettes,
market stalls, signposts, building and fountain collisions are generated by
`village_presentation.gd`. `tools/build_audio.py` synthesizes original ambience
and short attack, hit and fire cues without third-party samples.

## Limits

This is a playable opening area, not final art approval or a completed MMO.
Animated mesh pivots are a lightweight stylized solution, not a full character
animation library. Android device performance still needs physical testing.
The material crops are not mathematically seam-corrected; check repeats when
creating larger buildings. Forest zone geography is compressed into this first
test location and will need separate original scene mappings when expanded.
